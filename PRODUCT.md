## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are horse owners in Córdoba, Argentina, who need veterinary care for their equines (checkups, vaccinations, deworming, dental treatment, emergencies). Secondary users are owners of dogs and cats seeking routine veterinary services.

## Product Purpose

Clínica Veterinaria Hippos is a veterinary clinic offering care for horses, dogs, and cats in Córdoba, Argentina. The website's purpose is to present services, build trust, and capture appointment requests through a contact form and WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Specialized equine veterinary care in a region where it is uncommon, complemented by routine care for small pets. The clinic differentiates through hands-on equine expertise and 24/7 emergency availability.

## Operating Context

Local clinic serving the Córdoba area. Services include equine checkups, vaccinations, deworming, dental treatments, and emergencies; plus routine care, vaccination, sterilization, basic surgery, and clinical analysis for dogs and cats.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static-first marketing site (Next.js + Tailwind) with a contact form endpoint.
- Assets available: logo (blue background), four clinic photos (foto1–foto4), WhatsApp contact.
- No real email delivery configured yet; form submissions are logged server-side.
- Phone, email, and address shown are placeholders to be replaced with real data.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual identity uses the blue from the logo (#053457) as primary color.
- Tone: professional, warm, and close to the client.
